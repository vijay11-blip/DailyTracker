📅 Add Due

The month box is the first month this bill is due — for quarterly/yearly bills (insurance, property tax) it sets which months it repeats in. A day like 31 automatically becomes the last day in shorter months. Unpaid bills from earlier months stay listed as overdue until you mark them paid.

📅 Your Dues

💳 Add Credit Card

Fill in either a fixed Due day, or a Billing day + days until due. Expenses you log with method Card and pick this card are added to its outstanding automatically. Use + Payment for payments (optionally from a bank account), and New Statement when your next bill arrives.

💳 Your Cards

📉 Add Loan

The date box is your first EMI date. Leave "EMI" empty to use the standard reducing-balance EMI. For a loan you're already repaying, leave "EMIs paid" empty and every EMI dated before today is counted as paid (or type the exact number).
